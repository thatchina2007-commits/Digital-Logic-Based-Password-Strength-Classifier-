3-Bit Comparator Decoder
S₂ S₁ S₀ Logic

Hardware decoding logic applied to the 3-bit binary adder output $(S_2 S_1 S_0)_2$:

Weak Condition (Score ≤ 1): W = S₂' · S₁'
Medium Condition (Score 2 or 3): M = S₂' · S₁
Strong Condition (Score = 4): S = S₂ · S₁' · S₀'
Very Strong (Score = 5): V = S₂ · S₁' · S₀
Note: Since the maximum sum is $5 = (101)_2$, states $(110)_2$ and $(111)_2$ are **Don't Care conditions ($d$)** in the K-Map, simplifying logic implementation!
